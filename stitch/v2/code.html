A QR (Quick Response) code is a two-dimensional matrix barcode invented to store optical data. Unlike traditional 1D barcodes that only hold numbers, QR codes can store complex URLs, WiFi passwords, contact vCards, and encrypted identifiers that any modern smartphone camera can scan instantly.
Creating a QR code on SmartQRCraft takes under 30 seconds: simply pick your QR type (e.g., URL or WiFi), enter your content, personalize colors or upload your brand logo, and click 'Download PNG' or 'SVG Vector'.
