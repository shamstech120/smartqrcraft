How a WhatsApp QR code works

The code stores a wa.me link, which is WhatsApp's official click-to-chat address. When someone scans it, WhatsApp opens a chat with your number. If you added a message, it appears in their text box ready to send.

The person does not need to add you to their contacts, and you do not need to share your number in plain text. This works with both a personal WhatsApp account and the WhatsApp Business app.

How to create a WhatsApp QR code

Number format matters, so read step 2 carefully.

  1. Decide which number customers should message. It must be registered on WhatsApp.
  2. Enter the number with the country code and digits only, no plus sign, spaces or dashes. For an Indian number, 919876543210 means +91 98765 43210.
  3. Optional: add a pre-filled message such as "Hi, I saw your QR code and want to order".
  4. Choose a color, add the frame text you want, and download the PNG or SVG.
  5. Scan it from another phone to confirm the chat opens with the right number.

Tips for using WhatsApp QR codes for business

A few habits keep the chat useful for both sides.

  • Use a pre-filled message per campaign, for example "Poster A: I want the 2BHK details", so you know where the chat came from.
  • Answer during stated hours and say those hours on the poster so people know what to expect.
  • Consider WhatsApp Business for a business profile, quick replies and labels.
  • Do not put a personal number on a public code unless you are comfortable receiving messages from strangers.
  • Print at least about 1 inch (2.5 cm) wide, larger on boards, and test before printing.
