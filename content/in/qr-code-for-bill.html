How to add a payment QR code to a bill

  1. Enter your UPI ID and business name.
  2. Type the bill total in Amount and the bill or invoice number in Payment note.
  3. Download the PNG and place it on the bill in Word, Excel, Google Docs or your billing software.
  4. When the payment arrives, the note shows which bill was paid.

Safety tips

  • Always confirm the payment in your own UPI app or bank before marking a bill paid. A screenshot is not proof.
  • Print your business name next to the code so customers can see who they are paying.
  • Never ask customers for a UPI PIN except inside their own UPI app when they pay.
