How to add a logo to a QR code

It takes about a minute and no design software.

  1. Enter the link the code should open.
  2. Switch on Center Logo and upload a square PNG or JPG. A logo on a white or solid background works best.
  3. Choose your brand color, keeping the code dark against a light background.
  4. Download the PNG or SVG. Transparent PNG and SVG are available under the download options.
  5. Test the final file with two phones before you print.

Why a logo does not break the QR code

QR codes carry built-in error correction: extra data that lets a scanner rebuild parts that are hidden or damaged. A logo hides part of the pattern, and error correction fills the gap. When you turn on a logo, this tool raises the error correction level automatically.

The catch is that error correction has a limit. A small logo is safe, and a big one can make the code unreadable. That is why the logo here stays small and centered.

Tips for logo QR codes

Design should never cost you scans.

  • Keep the logo simple and centered. Fine detail disappears at small sizes.
  • Use strong contrast. Pale brand colors and light-on-dark codes are risky.
  • Shorten the link for a simpler pattern.
  • Leave a blank margin around the code.
  • Test on the real material such as your printed package or sign.
