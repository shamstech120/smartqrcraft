How to print your QR code template

  1. Choose a template and fill in what the QR code should do.
  2. Type your heading, business name and small print, and pick an accent color.
  3. Click Print / Save as PDF. In the print dialog, choose Actual size or 100% scale.
  4. To get a PDF, choose Save as PDF as the printer. Send that PDF to a print shop for card stock or lamination.
  5. Scan one printed copy with a phone before you print the rest.

Folding a table tent

Print on thick paper (160 g/m² or more) if your printer accepts it. Fold along the dashed line so both halves face out. The top half is printed upside down on purpose, so it reads the right way up after folding. Tape the bottom edges together or slide it into a clear acrylic stand.

Printing on label sheets

Label sheets differ between brands, even when they have the same number of labels. Print one page on plain paper first and hold it against your label sheet in front of a light. If it lines up, print on the labels. If not, choose a different layout or adjust your printer's margins.

  • 24 per sheet (70 × 37 mm) and 65 per sheet (38 × 21 mm) match common A4 address and mini label sizes.
  • Tiny labels only work with short content, such as a short link.
  • Use waterproof or vinyl labels for outdoors and anything that gets wet.
Tips

Tips for printed QR codes

Keep the code dark

A dark code on white scans best. Use your brand color for the heading and border instead.

Say what happens

"Scan to see the menu" gets more scans than a bare code.

Protect it

Laminate signs and table tents that will be handled or wiped.

WiFi passwords

Untick "Print the password" if you do not want it visible on the card; the code still contains it.

Check for tampering

In public places, check now and then that nobody has stuck another code over yours.
