How to make a Cash App QR code

Your $Cashtag is all you need.

  1. Open Cash App and find your $Cashtag on your profile.
  2. Type it into the field above. The $ sign is optional.
  3. Choose colors and, if you like, add your logo.
  4. Download the PNG or the SVG.
  5. Scan the finished code with another phone and check that your Cash App page opens.

Cash App also has its own QR code inside the app. Use a custom code when you need branded colors, a logo or a crisp file for printing.

How it works

The QR code contains your public Cash App link, which looks like cash.app/$yourcashtag. Scanning it opens your page in Cash App or the browser, where the payer enters the amount.

This tool only creates the picture. It does not handle any money and does not see your account.

Payment QR code safety

Codes that take money need extra care.

  • Only you should print your code. A fake sticker placed over a real one can send payments to a stranger.
  • Check the name. Scan your own code and confirm your name and photo appear before you print copies.
  • Scanning is for paying. If someone asks you to scan a code to receive money, it is a scam.
  • Send a small test payment from another account before you rely on the code.
  • Use a $Cashtag you plan to keep. If you change it, printed codes stop working.
