How to make a Bitcoin payment QR code

  1. Copy a receive address from your wallet. Addresses start with bc1, 1 or 3.
  2. Paste it above. The code only appears when the address format is valid.
  3. Optionally set an amount in BTC and a label such as your shop name.
  4. Download the code, then scan it with your own wallet to check the address matches character by character before you share it.

What the fields do

FieldEffect in the payer's wallet
AddressWhere the Bitcoin is sent. Required.
AmountPre-fills the amount in BTC. The payer can usually change it.
LabelShows who is being paid, for example a shop name.
MessageA note, such as an order number.

Safety notes

  • Always verify the address after scanning your own code. A wrong character sends funds that cannot be recovered.
  • Watch for stickers over your code. Scammers sometimes paste their own code over a public one.
  • This page never sees your keys. A receive address is public information; never put a private key or seed phrase in a QR code.
  • Lightning invoices are different. This generator makes on-chain BIP21 codes only.
