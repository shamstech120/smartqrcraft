How to Scan a QR Code on Android

Most Android phones can scan QR codes without an extra app. The exact buttons depend on the brand and Android version, so here are all the built-in ways.

Method 1: the camera app

  1. Open the camera and point it at the QR code.
  2. Hold steady. A link or button appears on screen.
  3. Tap it to open the link or action.

On Samsung Galaxy phones, if nothing appears, open the camera settings and turn on Scan QR codes. On Pixel phones the camera uses Google Lens suggestions; make sure they are switched on in camera settings.

Method 2: the Quick Settings tile

  1. Swipe down twice from the top of the screen to open Quick Settings.
  2. Tap Scan QR code. If it is not there, tap the pencil (edit) icon and drag it in.
  3. Point at the code.

This tile is available on many phones running Android 13 or later; some brands use their own name for it.

Method 3: Google Lens (also for screenshots)

  1. Open the Google app or Google Lens and tap the camera icon, or long-press the Home button or navigation handle on phones that support it.
  2. Point at a code, or choose a screenshot or photo from your gallery.
  3. Tap the link Lens finds.

You can also open a screenshot in Google Photos and tap Lens. Or use our online QR code scanner and choose the image; it runs on your phone and nothing is uploaded.
