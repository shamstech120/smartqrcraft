Free QR Code Widget for Your Website

Paste two lines of HTML and your page shows a QR code of itself. Desktop visitors scan it to continue on their phone. It works on any site, including WordPress, Shopify, Wix and plain HTML.

How to add the QR code widget

  1. Choose the options above: automatic (the current page) or a fixed link, size, color and caption.
  2. Click Copy code.
  3. Paste the code into your page where the QR code should appear, for example a sidebar, footer or product page.
  4. Open the page and scan the code with your phone to check it.

Privacy and performance

The widget is one small script with no cookies, no tracking and no calls to other services. The QR code is drawn in the visitor's browser, so page addresses are never sent to us or anyone else. The script loads with async, so it does not slow down the rest of your page.
