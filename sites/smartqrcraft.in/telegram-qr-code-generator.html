How to make a Telegram QR code

  1. Find your public username in Telegram under Settings (it starts with @). For a channel or group, use its public link name.
  2. Type it above, with or without the @. You can also paste a t.me link.
  3. Optionally add a message that is pre-filled when someone opens a chat with you.
  4. Style the code, download it and test it with Telegram installed.

Private groups and invite links

Private groups use invite links such as t.me/+AbCdEf. Paste the full invite link into the URL generator instead. Anyone with the code can join, so treat it like the invite link itself, and revoke the link in Telegram if it leaks.

Tips

  • Usernames must be public. Without a username, a QR code cannot open a chat with you.
  • Telegram also has built-in QR codes for profiles, but they cannot be restyled or given your logo. This page lets you design and print your own.
  • Pre-filled messages work on recent Telegram versions; older apps just open the chat.
