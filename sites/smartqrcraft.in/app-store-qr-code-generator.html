How to make an app download QR code

  1. Open your app's page in the App Store or Google Play and copy the link.
  2. Paste it above and choose a frame such as "Get the app".
  3. Download PNG or SVG, and test it on the matching phone type.

One code for iPhone and Android?

A store link opens only its own store. An App Store link on an Android phone opens a web page, not Google Play. Here are your options:

OptionHow it worksGood for
Two codes side by sideOne labelled App Store, one labelled Google PlayPrint where there is room; no setup
A download page on your siteOne code links to yourapp.com/download, which shows both badges or detects the phoneOne code, full control, works forever
A single storeIf almost all your users are on one platformSimple, but excludes the others

Best practice: link to your own download page

Making a static code of yourapp.com/download means the printed code never breaks, even if your store links change. That page can show both store badges, or a developer can redirect automatically based on the visitor's device.
