Helpful details for bug reports

It helps if you tell us:

  • which page you were on
  • your device and browser
  • what you expected and what happened
  • the content you entered, if it is not private (never send passwords or payment details)

Things we cannot help with

We cannot change a QR code you have already printed, because static codes hold their content inside the pattern. We are also not able to help with problems at other companies, such as payment apps or social networks. If a QR code you found is suspicious, do not open it.
