How to make a colored QR code

  1. Enter your link or details.
  2. In Color & Shape, pick a color or type your brand's hex code.
  3. For a two-tone look, choose a gradient or a different color for the corner squares.
  4. Watch for the low-contrast warning. If it appears, pick a darker code color or a lighter background.
  5. Download and test the printed version before you print many copies.

Three rules for colored QR codes

  • Dark on light. A white QR code on a black or blue background looks striking, but some scanners cannot read inverted codes.
  • Keep enough contrast. Aim for a contrast ratio of at least 4:1; the generator warns below 3:1.
  • Check print colors. Bright screen colors can print lighter in CMYK. Test a printed sample.
