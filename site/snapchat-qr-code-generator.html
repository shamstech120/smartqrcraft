Snapcode or custom QR code?

Snapchat has its own scannable image called a Snapcode, and it works inside the Snapchat app. A custom QR code is different: it is a standard QR code that any phone camera can read, and it opens your Snapchat profile link.

Use a custom QR code when you want a code that works with every camera, matches your brand colors, or prints sharply at any size.

How to make a Snapchat QR code

Your public add link has the form snapchat.com/add/yourusername.

  1. Find your username in Snapchat. You can create the link by adding it after snapchat.com/add/.
  2. Paste the full link into the field above.
  3. Choose your colors and a frame such as "Add me on Snapchat".
  4. Download the PNG or SVG.
  5. Scan it with another phone to check that it opens your profile.

Tips

A few notes keep the code useful.

  • Keep your username stable. If you change it, the old link breaks and printed codes stop working.
  • Check your privacy settings. Decide who can contact you before you share your profile widely.
  • Avoid personal details on public flyers, and treat any public code as visible to everyone.
  • Size: at least about 1 inch (2.5 cm) for cards and stickers, larger on banners.
