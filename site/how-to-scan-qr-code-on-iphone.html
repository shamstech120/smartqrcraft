How to Scan a QR Code on iPhone

You do not need an app. The iPhone camera reads QR codes on its own, and there are two more built-in ways for photos and screenshots.

Method 1: the Camera app

  1. Open the Camera app and choose Photo mode.
  2. Point the rear camera at the QR code and hold steady for a second. You do not need to take a picture.
  3. A yellow link or button appears above or below the code. Tap it to open the link, join WiFi, save a contact or start a call.

Nothing appears? Go to Settings > Camera and make sure Scan QR Codes is on.

Method 2: Code Scanner in Control Center

The Code Scanner opens a dedicated scanner with a flashlight button, handy in dark restaurants.

  1. Swipe down from the top-right corner (or up from the bottom on iPhones with a Home button) to open Control Center.
  2. Tap Code Scanner. If you cannot see it, add it in Control Center settings.
  3. Point it at the code; the result opens automatically.

Method 3: from a photo or screenshot

When the QR code is already on your iPhone, for example in a screenshot, email or chat:

  1. Open the image in the Photos app.
  2. On recent versions of iOS, a QR code button appears, or you can press and hold on the code to get the link.
  3. If your iPhone does not offer that, open our online QR code scanner in Safari, tap Choose image and pick the screenshot. It is read on your phone; nothing is uploaded.

Before you tap: a safety check

The preview button shows the website name. Read it before you open it. Be careful with codes on parking meters, stickers and unexpected mail, and never enter passwords or bank details after scanning a code you do not trust. Our QR code safety guide explains common scams.
