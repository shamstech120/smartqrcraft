How to create a Venmo QR code

The code points to your public Venmo profile link. You only need your username.

  1. Open the Venmo app and find your username. It appears on your profile under your name.
  2. Type it into the field above, with or without the @ sign.
  3. Pick colors and a style. A dark code on a light background scans best.
  4. Optional: add a logo or the frame text "Pay me on Venmo".
  5. Download the PNG for screens and documents, or the SVG for print.
  6. Scan it from another phone and check that it opens your profile.

The Venmo app also has its own personal QR code. A custom code is useful when you want your brand colors, a logo, or a sharp file for printing.

What happens when someone scans it

The QR code contains your Venmo profile link. Scanning it opens your profile in the Venmo app if it is installed, or in a browser. From there, the person chooses the amount and sends the payment from their own account.

This tool does not process payments and never sees your money or your login. It only turns a link into a picture.

Keep your Venmo QR code safe

A payment code is only as safe as the place you put it.

  • Only you should print your code. A fake sticker placed over a real one can send payments to a stranger.
  • Check the name. Scan your own code and confirm your name and photo appear before you print copies.
  • Scanning is for paying. If someone asks you to scan a code to receive money, it is a scam.
  • Send a small test payment from another account before you rely on the code.
