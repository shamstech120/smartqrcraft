How to make a heart QR code

  1. Choose Text for a hidden message, or URL for a song, video or photo album link.
  2. Type your message. Short messages make simpler codes that print well.
  3. Change the frame text under the heart, for example "SCAN ME" or "FOR YOU".
  4. Pick a color: red, pink or gold all work if they are dark enough.
  5. Download PNG or JPG for sharing, or SVG for printing on a card.

Printing a heart QR code

The heart frame adds space around the code, so print the whole design at least 4 cm (1.6 in) wide for cards and larger for posters. Keep the code itself dark on white. The heart outline and text are decoration; the square code in the middle is what the phone reads. Test one printed copy before you print more.
