How to find your Google review link

The QR code is only as good as the link inside it. You need the link that opens the "write a review" form for your business, not just your Maps listing. There are two reliable ways to get it.

  1. From your Business Profile (easiest): sign in to your Google Business Profile, open the reviews section, and choose the option to get more reviews. Google shows a shareable link and its own QR code there. Copy the link.
  2. With your Place ID: look up your business in Google's Place ID Finder and copy the ID (it begins with ChIJ). Then build the link: https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=YOUR_PLACE_ID.

Paste that link above. Before you print anything, open the link on your own phone to confirm it shows the review form for the right location.

How to create a Google review QR code

Rules to follow when asking for Google reviews

A QR code makes it easy to ask, but Google has rules about how you ask. Check Google's current review policy, and keep these basics in mind.

  • Do not offer incentives. Discounts, freebies or entries in exchange for reviews go against Google's policies.
  • Do not gate reviews. Asking only happy customers to review, and routing unhappy ones elsewhere, is discouraged. Ask everyone.
  • Do not write or buy reviews. Fake reviews can lead to removal of the listing's reviews.
  • Reply to reviews. Responding shows new customers you are active, whether the review is good or bad.
