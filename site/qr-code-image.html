How to make a QR code image

  1. Choose what the code should do: open a link, join WiFi, save a contact and more.
  2. Enter your details and style the code: colors, shapes, frame text and logo.
  3. Pick a download size. 1024 px suits screens and documents; 2048 px or more suits large prints.
  4. Click Download JPG or Download PNG. The image is saved to your device.

A QR code with your photo in it

Want the code to look like a picture? Open the Logo tab and turn on Picture QR (photo behind the code). Your photo shows through small dots while the code still scans. Picture QR codes download as PNG or JPG.

A QR code cannot store the photo itself, because it only holds a few thousand characters. To share a photo, upload it to a service such as Google Photos or Google Drive, copy the share link and make a QR code of that link.

Tips for QR code images

  • Do not resize small images up. Download at the size you need; enlarging a small JPG makes it blurry.
  • Keep a white border around the code when you place it on a photo or colored background.
  • Screenshots work, but a downloaded file is sharper and has no phone status bar.
  • Test before sharing: scan the saved image with a second phone, or check it with the QR code tester.
