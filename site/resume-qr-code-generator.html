How to add a QR code to your resume

It helps when the QR code opens something useful and easy to read on a phone.

  1. Decide what to link: a portfolio, your LinkedIn profile, an online resume or a project page.
  2. Paste the link into the field above.
  3. Choose a dark color that fits your resume design.
  4. Download the PNG or SVG and place it in the header or footer, about 2 cm (0.8 in) or larger.
  5. Save your resume as a PDF, then scan the code from the PDF to check it works.

A warning about applicant tracking systems

Many companies use software to read resumes before a person sees them, and these systems often cannot read QR codes. If your resume is uploaded online, always print the link as text too, for example under the code.

The code is most useful on printed resumes, at career fairs, on business cards and in portfolios, where a person will see it.

Tips for a resume QR code

Keep it professional and useful.

  • Do not replace your contact details with a code. Keep email and phone as text.
  • Use a clean, plain design. A dark code on white looks professional and scans best.
  • Do not link to a private page or something that needs a login.
  • Test the printed copy before a career fair. Check the size and contrast.
  • Update the destination, not the code. Keep the link the same and change the content behind it.
